# Infrastructure wiki

## Why I started

Honestly? A friend called me a noob. Classic larping, but it stuck with me, and I decided to actually go and learn.

What I found was Nix. And Nix gave me such an insane amount of power over my own machines that I couldn't stop. I started rebuilding everything I own with it, piece by piece, until the whole setup you see in this wiki existed.

I won't pretend it's easy. For a newcomer it's honestly a bit too hard. The core idea takes real technical ability, some knowledge, and a bit of *rebellion* to even grasp. You have to first believe that things on this world can run **better** than they do right now under a handful of big corporations. That belief is the hard part.

I don't want to prop up companies that are involved, directly or indirectly, in manipulating people or in killing them. So instead of complaining about it, I started building my own ecosystem, one I actually control, one that answers to me and not to a quarterly report.

I hope it lasts until the day I die.

## About this wiki

This page is the one I write by hand: the big picture, the *why*, and where a newcomer should start.

Everything else (Architecture, Hosts, Services, Endpoints) is **auto-generated from the NixOS configuration** on every commit, so it is always current.
